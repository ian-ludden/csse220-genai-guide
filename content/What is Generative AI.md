# What is Generative Artificial Intelligence?
## Wait, what's AI? 
First, we should note that *Artificial Intelligence* (AI) is tricky to define. As its name suggests, it refers to "the capability of computational systems to perform tasks typically associated with human intelligence" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence)). But often, an application of AI [ceases to be labeled as AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_effect) once we get used to it being present. 
## What are we generating? 
The *generative* modifer in genAI refers to creating new content: text (including code), images, and videos. One of the powerful features of modern genAI tools is that they can handle inputs in [natural language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_language_processing) (in contrast to carefully formatted configuration files).
