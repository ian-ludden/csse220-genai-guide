# Why are we experimenting with GenAI in CSSE 220?
*This description is borrowed from course materials of Sam Lau at UCSD.*

Seeing the capabilities of genAI tools in this course will:
- Prepare you to use genAI effectively *and* safely in your careers (in software engineering, or anywhere else)
- Give you a competitive edge in the job market
- Help you solve real-world programming challenges
- Allow you to automate repetitive tasks involved in programming (freeing you to focus on more strategic challenges)
