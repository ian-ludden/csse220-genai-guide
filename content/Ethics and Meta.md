As you know, genAI tools are powerful. Part of the reason we are using genAI tools in this course is to practice wielding them responsibly (obligatory [Uncle Ben reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/With_great_power_comes_great_responsibility)). This page collects perspectives from industry and academia on genAI tools, especially in the context of software development and computer science education. 

I share these perspectives not to endorse all of them and impose them upon you, but to encourage you to think critically about the role of genAI tools in software development, your learning, and society as a whole. 

If you come across resources you think worth adding here, please let me know.
